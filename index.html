Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cold Coffee Club is developed as an offline-first mobile game. Your privacy is paramount, and this policy outlines our data practices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). The game does not require user accounts, registration, or logins.

2. Device Storage

All game progress, statistics, unlocks, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using standard local storage (localStorage). This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to any external servers or third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Cold Coffee Club does not include any third-party tracking tools, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

4. Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders or game alerts, these are scheduled and delivered locally by your operating system. No notification tokens or device identifiers are transmitted externally.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
